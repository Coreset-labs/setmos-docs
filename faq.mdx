## What is Setmos?

Setmos brings premium TradFi real-world asset categories on-chain through Set tokens.

## Who is Setmos built for?

Setmos is built for individuals, institutions, banks, fintech APIs, and on-chain investors.

## What Set tokens are expected at launch?

The current launch set includes setTSY, setMM, setCRD, setLQD, setPCP, and setG.

## Is Setmos available to everyone?

Access to certain Set tokens may vary by jurisdiction, onboarding status, KYC/AML review, sanctions screening, wallet review, and product-level requirements.

## Is the app an investment offer?

No. The app and docs are product information and previews unless otherwise stated. They do not constitute an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security, token, fund interest, or investment product.

## What is the settlement rail?

Setmos is designed around USDC settlement for launch products.

## What network does Setmos use?

Setmos uses the Stellar blockchain as core infrastructure.

## Can Set tokens be used beyond holding yield exposure?

Set tokens are designed to become usable as collateral across selected DeFi venues and protocols, subject to integration and risk review.

## Where are final product terms defined?

Final terms are defined in product-level legal, subscription, offering, custody, onboarding, and methodology documents.
