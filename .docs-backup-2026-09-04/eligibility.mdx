Setmos is built for individuals, institutions, banks, and fintech platforms. Product availability may vary by jurisdiction, onboarding status, KYC/AML review, sanctions screening, wallet review, and product requirements.

Eligibility may be evaluated before participation is approved and may be re-evaluated over time.

## Eligibility principles

Setmos related products and services are intended only for users who satisfy all applicable legal, regulatory, compliance, onboarding, wallet, and product requirements.

Users may be prohibited or restricted from subscribing for, acquiring, holding, transferring, pledging, or redeeming Setmos related products if they are located in, organized under, acting from, acting for the benefit of, or otherwise connected to a prohibited or restricted jurisdiction, person, entity, wallet, or activity.

## Prohibited jurisdictions

Setmos does not intend to make Setmos related products available to persons located or resident in, entities organized under the laws of, or entities with a principal place of business in jurisdictions that are prohibited by Setmos policy, sanctions programs, product documentation, or applicable law.

The prohibited jurisdiction list may include, without limitation:

- Afghanistan
- Belarus
- Canada
- Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson, Zaporizhzhia, and Sevastopol regions
- Cuba
- Democratic People's Republic of Korea
- Iran
- Libya
- Myanmar
- Russia
- Somalia
- South Sudan
- Sudan
- Syria
- United States and its states, territories, possessions, and federal districts, where restricted by product documents or applicable law

<Note>
  US and Global products may follow different eligibility, documentation, and participation paths. A person may be eligible for one route and ineligible for another.
</Note>

## US persons

Setmos Global products are not intended for US persons or persons acting for the account or benefit of US persons where participation would be prohibited by product documents, securities laws, sanctions laws, onboarding requirements, or applicable law.

For purposes of Global products, restricted status may apply to persons located in the United States, persons placing orders from within the United States, US persons as defined under applicable securities rules, and persons acting for the account or benefit of such persons.

## Sanctions and high-risk activity

Setmos may deny, suspend, restrict, or terminate participation where a person, entity, wallet, beneficial owner, controller, counterparty, or related activity is associated with sanctions, prohibited activity, high-risk wallet activity, illicit finance indicators, or other compliance concerns.

Restrictions may also apply where prohibited or restricted persons directly or indirectly own or control a significant portion of an entity.

## Restricted jurisdictions

Some jurisdictions may not be prohibited outright but may require additional investor status, certifications, declarations, financial thresholds, professional investor status, accredited investor status, qualified purchaser status, or equivalent local eligibility evidence.

Restricted persons may be required to provide documents or certifications showing that they satisfy the relevant eligibility requirements before participation is approved.

## Ongoing review

Approval is not permanent. Setmos and its compliance or service providers may re-screen users, wallets, beneficial owners, and transactions.

Participation may be limited, delayed, suspended, or revoked if continued participation may violate:

- applicable law, rule, regulation, or sanctions restriction;
- governing documents for a product or token;
- wallet policy or transfer controls;
- onboarding certifications;
- Setmos operating policies;
- product requirements.
