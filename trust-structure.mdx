Setmos tokenizes premium real-world asset categories for onchain finance. The product layer is designed around blockchain settlement, reporting, custody coordination, wallet controls, and future utility.

<img src="/images/investor-flow-setmos.png" alt="Setmos investor access flow and custody structure" />

## Role of Setmos

Setmos creates and operates the product experience. The platform may support onboarding workflows, wallet policy, product navigation, reporting views, token operations, custody coordination, and product user experience.

Setmos is designed to make real-world asset exposure easier to access, monitor, settle, and use onchain.

## Product composition

Each asset corresponds to a product category such as treasuries, money market funds, private credit, liquidity strategies, alternative income, or gold. Product documentation may describe asset composition, service providers, pricing methodology, custody structure, liquidity mechanics, and risk factors.

## Custody model

Setmos uses Stellar as core infrastructure. Custody, settlement, and operational arrangements may involve Setmos, third-party custody providers, product-specific service providers, and wallet controls.

## Onchain and offchain

| Layer | Example responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Onchain | Token representation, wallet controls, transfer restrictions, USDC settlement flows, and future utility integrations |
| Offchain | KYC/AML, jurisdiction review, sanctions screening, product documentation, custody operations, reconciliation, and reporting sources |

## Reporting and updates

Setmos may display yield, price, fund size, settlement, network, allocation, and product status. Metrics may come from product documentation, public market data, protocol data, reporting sources, or Setmos-controlled reporting layers.

Metrics may be delayed, rounded, estimated, or subject to source availability. Product documents control where a displayed metric conflicts with final product reporting.
