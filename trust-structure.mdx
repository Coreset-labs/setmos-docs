Setmos brings premium real-world asset categories on-chain through Set tokens. The Setmos product layer is designed around blockchain-based settlement, reporting, custody coordination, wallet controls, and future utility across on-chain finance.

<img src="/images/investor-flow-setmos.png" alt="Setmos investor access flow and custody structure" />

## Role of Setmos

Setmos creates and operates the Set token product experience. Setmos may support onboarding workflows, wallet policy, product navigation, reporting views, token operations, custody coordination, and product-level user experience.

Setmos is designed to make real-world asset exposure easier to access, monitor, settle, and use on-chain.

## Product composition

Each Set token corresponds to a product category such as treasuries, money market funds, private credit, liquidity strategies, alternative income, or gold. Product-level documentation may describe asset composition, service providers, pricing methodology, custody structure, liquidity mechanics, and risk factors.

## Custody model

Set tokens use Stellar as core infrastructure. Custody, settlement, and operational arrangements may involve Setmos, third-party custody providers, product-specific service providers, and wallet controls.

## On-chain and off-chain

| Layer | Example responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| On-chain | Set token representation, wallet controls, transfer restrictions, USDC settlement flows, and future utility integrations |
| Off-chain | KYC/AML, jurisdiction review, sanctions screening, product documentation, custody operations, reconciliation, and reporting sources |

## Reporting and updates

Setmos may display yield, price, fund size, settlement, network, allocation, and product status. Metrics may come from product documentation, public market data, protocol data, reporting sources, or Setmos-controlled reporting layers.

Metrics may be delayed, rounded, estimated, or subject to source availability. Product documents control where a displayed metric conflicts with final product-level reporting.
