# Premium real-world assets, brought on-chain

Setmos creates Set tokens for individuals, institutions, and on-chain investors, bringing treasuries, money market funds, private credit, alternative income, and gold into blockchain-based finance.

Set tokens are designed to make premium real-world asset categories easier to hold, settle, report, and eventually use across on-chain financial applications.

## How Setmos works

Setmos is designed around a simple flow: onboard, verify, fund, mint Set tokens, monitor positions, and redeem when available under product terms.

## Beyond yield

Setmos is building Set tokens for more than passive exposure. Set tokens are designed to become usable as collateral across selected DeFi venues and protocols, subject to integration and risk review.

Future utility areas may include collateral markets, lending venues, portfolio products, cross-chain interoperability, and fintech API integrations.

## Expansion roadmap

Setmos is expanding into tokenized real estate, bitcoin yield, and gold yield, with additional premium RWA categories planned over time.

## Product disclosures and methodology

Each Set token may include product-level documentation covering asset composition, pricing methodology, custody structure, liquidity mechanics, service provider roles, fees, and risk factors.

<Note>
  Access to certain Set tokens may vary by jurisdiction, onboarding status, wallet review, sanctions screening, and product-level requirements.
